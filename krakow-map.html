Pin shapes: circles are sights, rounded squares are food, diamonds are bars. Colour bands food by price, not cuisine. FREE DAYS DIFFER BY MUSEUM: National Museum branches (Czartoryski, Sukiennice) are free Tuesdays and shut Mondays; Museum of Kraków branches vary — Schindler's is free Monday, Rynek Underground Tuesday, Old Synagogue Monday, Eagle Pharmacy Wednesday. Anyone aged 7–26 with a student card pays 1zł at National Museum branches. Kraków uses the złoty, not the euro. Cash still matters: markets, milk bars and the sausage van are cash-only or cash-preferred. SCAM WARNING: on Szewska and Floriańska, being invited to a club by friendly strangers ends in a bill many times what you expected. Walk away. Dropped from this map: the Tytano / Dolne Młyny nightlife complex, which older guides still list — it closed in 2020 and is a building site until 2026–27.
