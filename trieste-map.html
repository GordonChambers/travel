Pin shapes: circles are sights, squares are food, diamonds are drink. Gold ring = do not miss. Colour bands food by price, so you can switch off the expensive pins. CHECK BEFORE YOU GO: the Opicina tram leaves from Piazza Dalmazia, not Piazza Oberdan, until the Oberdan works finish (~mid-2027). Kleine Berlin opens the last Friday of the month only, by email, cash only. The Faro della Vittoria is April-4 November, mostly Fri-Sun. Grotta Gigante is shut on non-holiday Mondays and guided only. Museums: Revoltella and Museo Joyce shut Tuesdays. Many restaurants shut Sunday or Monday. Osmize open a few days at a time - check osmize.com.
