Day Trips from Vienna

Everything here is a day out from Vienna, sorted by what it is rather than which direction it lies in. Journey times in the sub-labels are from a central Vienna station. Seasonal closures are IN CAPS and matter: Carnuntum shuts for winter, the Wachau boats run roughly April to October, and the storks leave in August. Research for this map is logged with the main Vienna guide. Place data and photos (c) Google and the photographers credited on each card; the photos are traveller submissions that could not be viewed while this map was built.
