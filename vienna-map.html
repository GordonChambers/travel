Pin shapes: circles are sights, rounded squares are food, diamonds are drinks. Food is banded by price, not cuisine. Closing days are IN CAPS in the meta line and matter more here than in most cities: many of the best Beisl shut at weekends and most museums shut on Monday or Tuesday.
