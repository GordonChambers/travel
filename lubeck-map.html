Pin shapes: circles are sights, rounded squares are food, diamonds are drinks. Closing days are in CAPS on each card — Lübeck shuts hard on Mondays (most museums) and Sundays (many kitchens). No photos in this build: the place-data source returned none, so cards are text only. Hours, prices and ratings were checked in August 2026 and move; the Buddenbrookhaus is a building site until roughly 2031. Sources: Lübeck city and tourism sites, the Lübecker Museen, the MICHELIN guide, German-language local blogs, Wikipedia, and Google place data.
