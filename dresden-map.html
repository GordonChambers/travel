Dresden museums mostly shut Mondays; the Historisches Grünes Gewölbe shuts Tuesdays and needs a separate timed ticket. Saxon Switzerland, as of 22 August 2026: a severe storm on 31 July damaged the Amselgrund at Kurort Rathen and the Felsenbühne open-air theatre is out of use; the Bastei, its bridge and the Schwedenlöcher are open. Felsenburg Neurathen has been closed since 2023. Low water has suspended most Elbe paddle-steamer lines. Malerweg stages 2, 3 and 5 are on signposted diversions. Bark beetle damage closes paths at short notice: ring the national park centre on 035022 50240 before a long walk.
